When a PWA Fits

Useful beats complicated.

A PWA can be a strong fit for calculators, dashboards, customer tools, internal workflows, wellness products, field tools, and focused SaaS-style experiences.

The right question is not “Can we build an app?” It is “Would an installable web app make this experience better?”
