Product Q&A

Straight answers about the apps.

The questions people actually ask about Toastid Tech apps — pricing, privacy, and what each one does. No sales pitch, just answers.

CopeWhat is Cope?
Cope is your private AI mental-health buddy — a judgment-free companion you can talk to anytime, day or night. No appointments, no waiting rooms. Just open the app and let it out. $12.99/month standalone, or $19.99/month with the full AI chat experience. Try Cope →
CopeIs my conversation with Cope private?
Yes. Cope is built private and confidential by design — what's said in Cope stays in Cope.
CopeIs Cope a replacement for therapy?
No. Cope is a wellness companion, not a medical provider — it doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If you're ever in crisis, reach out to a professional or call/text 988. Cope is the soft place to land in between.
BiteFactWhat is BiteFact?
Snap a photo of your plate and BiteFact's AI estimates the nutrition — calories, protein, the works. You confirm the portions, it handles the math. Plans: free manual entry, Plus at $12.99/mo, or the AI photo tier at $19.99/mo with a 3-day free trial. Try BiteFact →
SOPilotWhat does SOPilot do?
SOPilot turns the way you already work into a professional, role-assigned SOP in minutes — AI-drafted, auditor-ready. Your first 5 SOPs are free, then $9.99/month. Try SOPilot →
PulseMatrixWhat is PulseMatrix?
Six AI marketing tools in one command hub: ad scripts, scroll-stopping hooks, visuals, content calendars, and competitor counter-punches. Founding members pay $9.99 once — yours forever, no subscription. Try PulseMatrix →
PulseMatrixWhat does "bring your own key" mean?
You plug in your own AI provider API key, so your AI queries bill directly to your provider account at raw cost. Toastid Tech never marks it up — you pay nothing extra per query to us. Your key stays in your browser and never leaves your device.
AuraTideWhat is AuraTide?
Your AI-powered daily horoscope and manifestation companion — personalized readings, unlimited oracle rolls, compatibility insights, and tomorrow previews, tailored to your birth profile. $9.99/month or $59.99/year (save 50%). Try AuraTide →
Micro HabitsWhat is Micro Habits?
A dead-simple habit-tracking PWA — small daily wins, streaks, zero bloat. $24.99 one-time, yours forever.
CanvasFlowWhat is CanvasFlow?
Our premium eight-tool design PWA, built with graphic designers in mind. Currently in final testing — watch the products page for the launch.
GeneralDo I need to install anything to use your apps?
Nope. Every Toastid Tech app is a PWA — it runs right in your browser, and you can install it to your home screen in one tap if you want the full app feel. No app store, no 200MB download, no waiting.
GeneralHow do I pay?
Securely through PayPal — both subscriptions and one-time purchases. No card details ever touch our servers.
GeneralWhat is Toastid Cloud?
Our planned shared backend platform — one account and bring-your-own-key AI across every Toastid Tech app, cutting per-query costs for everyone. It's in active development now. Reach out if you want early access.

What happened in AI this week.

The week's AI headlines, translated for people who run businesses — what changed, and why it matters to you.

Models · Sep 22

Claude Opus 5.5 lands 40% cheaper

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 — frontier-grade performance priced 40% below its predecessor, plus new dual-use verification programs for sensitive applications.

Why it matters: Frontier AI keeps getting cheaper. That's exactly why bring-your-own-key tools like PulseMatrix are built the way they are — you ride the falling cost curve instead of paying someone else's markup.

Agents · Sep 25

OpenAI pushes GPT-6 Astra into agentic, computer-use work

OpenAI is moving its latest model beyond chat into autonomous agents that browse the web, operate computers, conduct research, and complete multi-step tasks.

Why it matters: The industry is shifting from chatbots to agents that do work. Businesses thinking in "what job can an agent do for me" terms now will be ahead of everyone still treating AI like a search box.
Source: IMFounder →
Models · Sep 25

Google expands Gemini 3.8 across voice, reasoning, and security

Google is pushing Gemini 3.8 into voice interfaces, deeper reasoning, cybersecurity, and agentic applications.

Why it matters: Voice AI is going mainstream fast. If your business still funnels every customer through a phone tree from 2009, the clock is ticking.
Source: IMFounder →
Consumer AI · Sep 25

Meta turns the personal AI agent mainstream

Meta's Muse is packaging the personal AI agent as a mainstream consumer product, paired with new AI glasses that push the concept beyond the phone.

Why it matters: Your customers are about to expect AI help everywhere. The bar for "responsive business" is moving — meet it with tools, not headcount.
Source: IMFounder →
Commerce · Sep 24

51% of US shoppers used AI to shop this month

NielsenIQ reports that 51% of US consumers used an AI tool to shop in the past month — AI-assisted buying has crossed the halfway mark.

Why it matters: Your customers are already shopping with AI. If your products aren't legible to AI — clear descriptions, structured info, real answers — you're invisible to half the market.
Source: Agile Brand Guide →
Security · Sep 23

"BragJack": malicious browser extensions can hijack AI agents

Security researcher Gal Weizman disclosed BragJack, a new attack class showing how a single malicious browser extension can seize control of AI agents in Chrome, Edge, and other browsers.

Why it matters: As AI agents go mainstream, they become targets. Keep your extensions minimal, your API keys private, and your agent permissions tight.
